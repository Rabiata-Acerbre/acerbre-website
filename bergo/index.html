Risk architecture

Bergo wins often. It can lose violently.

That asymmetry is the defining feature of these tests. A 90–96% win rate is not safety when the average loss is many times larger than the average win.

01 · Payoff asymmetry

Win rate is not enough.

On US100, the average loss was about 14 times the average win. The edge survives only while losing events remain sufficiently rare.

02 · Market dependence

Return and drawdown move together.

US500 showed little edge; Oil produced materially higher returns with 40% drawdown; Gold produced huge returns with an 89% equity drawdown.

03 · Test quality

Confidence is not uniform.

Oil and Gold use 99% real-tick histories. US100 and US500 report 73% real ticks, so their exact figures deserve more conservative interpretation.
