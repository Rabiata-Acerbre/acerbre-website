System thesis

A mechanical edge, not a behavioral story.

Grimmwood Apple is built around repeatable price-state conditions, defined timing, explicit risk and deterministic exits. It does not need a narrative about what market participants are feeling.

01 · Market

Designed for NQ

The strategy was conceived for Nasdaq-100 futures behavior. Its logic is aimed at a highly liquid, fast intraday market where timing and mechanical state transitions matter.

02 · Edge

Rules over interpretation

The system reacts to measurable market conditions. It is not a discretionary sentiment engine and does not attempt to infer fear, greed, positioning narratives or trader psychology.

03 · Execution

Fixed lifecycle

Entry qualification, sizing, stops, timed exits and state exits are codified. Once a condition is met, execution is mechanical rather than interpretive.
