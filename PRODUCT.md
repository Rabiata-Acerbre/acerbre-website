# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Acerbre serves three primary retail-market audiences: traders evaluating or buying Acerbre systems, investors evaluating the business and its research discipline, and brokers or other industry partners exploring collaboration.

## Product Purpose

Acerbre is the algorithmic-trading business of Rabiata LLC. It researches, develops, licenses, and supports retail trading systems, and also provides custom-system development, consulting, and education for the retail trading realm.

Success means visitors quickly understand what Acerbre is, can distinguish its products and risk profiles, and can either buy a product license or contact Acerbre directly.

## Positioning

Acerbre combines productized MetaTrader 5 algorithms with an explicit public treatment of risk, account-bound licensing infrastructure, and adjacent research, custom-system, consulting, and educational work for retail trading.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate product pages, historical strategy-tester evidence, risk disclosures, licensing mechanics, and the suitability of AppleTree or Bergo for their intended risk posture. Licensed products run in MetaTrader 5 and use online authorization.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public products currently include AppleTree and Bergo, with separate licenses and different risk profiles.
- Services include retail-market research, custom trading-system development, consulting, and education.
- Primary actions are buying or requesting a product license and emailing Acerbre.
- The site must not imply guaranteed returns, investment advice, discretionary account management, or results beyond the evidence already published.
- Trading-risk and historical-simulation disclosures must remain prominent.

## Brand Commitments

- Acerbre remains the trading-focused business identity of Rabiata LLC.
- Existing Acerbre logo and product artwork remain authoritative assets.
- Voice is analytical, direct, risk-aware, and commercially confident without hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing product and licensing pages: `appletree/index.html`, `bergo/index.html`, and `licensing/index.html`.
- Public Bergo MT5 Strategy Tester metrics and methodology disclosure in `bergo/index.html`.
- Existing Acerbre logo and AppleTree/Bergo pixel artwork under `assets/`.
- AppleTree MT5 Strategy Tester report supplied on 28 August 2026: US100p, M30, 1 June 2024–18 August 2026, US$100,000 initial deposit, 73% real ticks, US$20,328.29 net profit, 2.67% maximum equity drawdown, 2.04 profit factor, and 297 trades. Historical simulation only; the report input sets cost per lot to 0.0.
- No customer testimonials, broker partnerships, live-return claims, or external certifications are currently supplied; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Explain the system and its risk together.
- Make different products and services easy to distinguish.
- Let evidence carry more weight than promises.
- Convert interest into a license request or a direct conversation.
- Keep retail traders informed without presenting education as financial advice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website should remain keyboard accessible, responsive, legible under reduced-motion preferences, and understandable without relying on animation alone.
