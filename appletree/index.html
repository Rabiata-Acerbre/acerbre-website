Product definition

One NQ research branch, multiple internal decision modules.

AppleTree is the multi-sleeve branch of Acerbre's mechanical NQ family. The public description is structural rather than formulaic; research thresholds and signal formulas remain part of the compiled implementation.

01 · Signal layer

Independent entry logic

AppleTree contains three separately triggered intraday sleeves. Each sleeve has its own market condition, directional rules and permitted trading window. A sleeve can remain inactive while another qualifies.

02 · Risk layer

Shared risk scaling

The user selects a single risk percentage. AppleTree scales the internal sleeve risk budgets from the validated base configuration rather than exposing dozens of tuning parameters.

03 · State layer

Defined lifecycle rules

Entries, stops, state exits, timed exits and hard-flat behavior are part of the strategy implementation. Licensing is not used as a substitute for position-risk management.

Client surface

Deliberately constrained.

AppleTree is not distributed as a parameter playground. The commercial interface limits the user to the portfolio risk scale while validated strategy constants remain embedded in the build.
