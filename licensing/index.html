AppleTree

Keyless, account-bound authorization.

Operational behavior

Authorization state is separate from position state.

A licensing event decides whether new risk may be created. It does not become an ad-hoc liquidation signal for positions that already exist.

01 · ActiveNew exposure allowed

The account is authorized and the EA may process its normal entry logic.

02 · Temporary outageBounded grace

After valid authorization, temporary service or network failure may use the configured grace policy rather than turning connectivity loss into a trading decision.

03 · Revoked / expiredNew exposure blocked

The EA stops creating new positions. Existing positions continue under their normal management rules.

MT5 setup

One manual terminal permission.

MetaTrader 5 requires users to explicitly allow WebRequest access to the licensing origin. The EA cannot silently add this permission itself.

AppleTree setup
  1. Open Tools → Options → Expert Advisors.
  2. Enable WebRequest for the Rabiata licensing URL supplied with the product.
  3. Attach the compiled AppleTree EA to the intended MT5 chart and enable Algo Trading.

AppleTree derives the account identifier from the running terminal; there is no license key field to complete.

Bergo: its public licensing policy is intentionally not stated here yet. The Bergo page will be updated after that product's commercial specification is confirmed.

Infrastructure boundary

The licensing service authorizes software use. It does not hold broker credentials, place discretionary trades, or guarantee strategy performance.
