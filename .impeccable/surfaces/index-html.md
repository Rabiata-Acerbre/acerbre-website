THESIS: The homepage behaves like a market observatory: it welcomes visitors into the work, makes the risk visible beside the signal, and then opens the business one disciplined layer at a time.

OWN-WORLD: Acerbre owns a precise instrument-panel visual language built from midnight surfaces, steel-blue measurement marks, rare signal-red accents, square geometry, and dense pixel illustrations that connect the established AppleTree and Bergo artwork to the wider business.

STORY: Welcome and market signal → business identity and audiences → research, custom systems, consulting, and education → evidence-led method → AppleTree and Bergo → Bergo evidence → trader/investor/partner paths → buy or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo and restrained navigation, a direct statement about trading systems and risk, a concise explanation of Acerbre, buy and email actions, and an animated research-to-risk signal path over the market-observatory asset.

FORM: Dark navy and steel surfaces; Manrope display/body with DM Mono labels; square cards and controls; thin borders and restrained red; generated pixel-art instruments and market landscapes; asymmetrical editorial grids that collapse to a clear single column.

FINISH: Responsive at desktop and mobile, keyboard-accessible navigation and actions, reduced-motion support, readable content independent of animation state, ten optimized WebP assets, no horizontal overflow, and explicit historical-simulation/risk language.
